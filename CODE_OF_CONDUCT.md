# Code of Conduct

Companion should be a place where people can disagree rigorously without making
the work hostile. Be direct about code and product decisions, generous about
other people's intent, and specific enough that feedback can be acted on.

Expected behaviour includes:

- treating people with respect regardless of identity, background, experience,
  communication style, or employer;
- critiquing ideas and evidence rather than the person presenting them;
- protecting private repository data, credentials, and personal information;
- accepting a maintainer decision without harassment, even when continuing the
  technical discussion elsewhere;
- correcting mistakes openly and helping leave the project clearer than it was.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted in
project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

For an incident that should not be public, open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/moxxy-ai/companion/security/advisories/new)
with the title prefixed `Conduct report`. Include only the information needed to
investigate. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban participation. Reports are handled
as privately as the investigation allows, and retaliation against a reporter is
not accepted.
